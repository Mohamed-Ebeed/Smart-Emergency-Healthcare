# Risk Assessment Task

## Task Title
Conduct a risk assessment for the Smart Emergency Healthcare system.

## Objective
Identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks that may affect patient safety, system reliability, data security, and emergency response performance.

## Scope
- Patient triage and emergency detection
- Medical data collection and transmission
- Real-time alerts and notification systems
- System availability and uptime
- Data privacy and cybersecurity
- Integration with hospital teams and emergency workflows

## Risk Categories
### 1. Clinical Safety Risks
- Incorrect triage decision
- Delayed emergency response
- False alarms or missed alerts

### 2. Data and Privacy Risks
- Unauthorized access to patient records
- Data corruption or loss
- Insecure API or device communication

### 3. Operational Risks
- System downtime during emergency events
- Poor connectivity or device failure
- Limited staff training or misuse of the application

### 4. Compliance and Legal Risks
- Failure to follow healthcare data protection regulations
- Incomplete audit trails or documentation

## Risk Evaluation Criteria
- Severity: Low / Medium / High / Critical
- Likelihood: Rare / Unlikely / Possible / Likely / Almost Certain
- Impact on patient care and operational continuity
- Required mitigation actions
- Owner and review date

## Example Risk Register
| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Severity | Impact | Mitigation | Owner | Status |
|--------|------------------|------------|----------|--------|-----------|-------|--------|
| R-01 | Delayed alert delivery to emergency teams | Medium | High | Patient care delay | Use redundancy and monitor alert latency | Team Lead | Open |
| R-02 | Unauthorized access to patient data | Low | Critical | Privacy breach | Enforce role-based access and encryption | Security Lead | Open |
| R-03 | Device/network failure during emergency use | Medium | High | Service disruption | Add offline fallback and automatic retry logic | Engineering Team | Open |

## Required Actions
- Review risks with clinical and technical stakeholders
- Define mitigation and escalation procedures
- Document approval and monitoring plan
- Update this file as new risks are identified

## Acceptance Criteria
- All major risk areas are listed
- Each risk includes likelihood, impact, and mitigation
- Owner and action status are assigned
- Risk review is documented for future updates
